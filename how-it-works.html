On the morning

  • Follow the fasting time in your confirmation; drink water.
  • Wear sleeves that roll up.
  • Have a photo ID ready.
  • Most people feel a quick pinch and nothing after.

Once results are in

  • You get a text and an email when results are in.
  • Send your doctor a secure link or a PDF.
  • Ask the My Health assistant what a marker means. It explains results; it doesn't give medical advice.
  • If a result is at a critical level, it's flagged in My Health and our team contacts you.
